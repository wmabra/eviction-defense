SaaS Product Brief: Foreclosure Document Prep Assistant

Project Goal: Build a self-help document automation platform that helps homeowners facing foreclosure generate ready-to-file paperwork — answer to complaint, hardship affidavits, lender communications, and procedural checklists. Built on the same architecture and compliance model as the eviction-defense platform.

Core Legal Compliance Rule: This platform is strictly a self-help document assembly tool. It does NOT negotiate with lenders, contact courts or opposing counsel, draft pleadings requiring strategic legal judgment, provide legal advice, or represent the homeowner. It maps user-provided answers into pre-built document templates that the homeowner reviews, signs, and files themselves (pro se).

2. What the Customer Gets ($499–$799 Packet)

  1. Answer to Foreclosure Complaint — pre-filled response to the lender's summons, asserting defenses the homeowner selects from a plain-language checklist
  2. Motion to Dismiss — where applicable (lack of standing, failure to attach note, statute of limitations)
  3. Hardship Affidavit — detailed financial hardship statement for loan modification or loss mitigation applications
  4. Lender Communication Package — request for loan modification, request for payoff statement, dispute of accounting errors
  5. Loss Mitigation Application — pre-filled with financial data the homeowner provides
  6. Filing Checklist — step-by-step instructions for the county where the property is located
  7. Mediation/Hearing Prep Guide — what to bring, what to say, what to expect
  8. Foreclosure Timeline — state-specific timeline showing key deadlines and what happens at each stage
  9. HUD Housing Counselor Directory — local HUD-approved agencies for free additional help
  10. Bankruptcy Primer — when Chapter 13 might make sense, how to find a bankruptcy attorney
  11. Deadline Reminders — optional SMS/email alerts for answer deadlines, hearing dates, mediation dates

Step 1: Document Upload

  • Homeowner uploads the foreclosure summons, complaint, lis pendens, and any lender correspondence they've received.
  • OCR extracts key data: lender name, loan amount, property address, case number, filing date, court jurisdiction.
  • System identifies the state and county to route to the correct form templates and deadlines.

Step 2: AI Chatbot Intake

  • Chatbot collects: employment status, income, monthly expenses, hardship cause (job loss, medical, divorce, etc.), mortgage details, prior loss mitigation attempts.
  • Plain-language defense selection: "Did the lender attach the original promissory note?" → "Did you receive proper notice of default?" → etc.
  • System never advises which defenses to use — it presents options in plain English and the homeowner chooses.

Step 3: Payment

  • Same Accept.js + Authorize.net integration as eviction-defense.
  • Potential tiered pricing: Basic ($499 — answer + checklist), Complete ($699 — full packet), Premium ($799 — includes deadline reminders & status dashboard).

Step 4: Document Generation

  • Same PDF filling architecture: fillable widgets for digital forms, coordinate overlay for scanned/static forms, ReportLab for generated documents.
  • State-specific templates loaded from config, matching the eviction-defense pattern.

Step 5: Delivery

  • Instant download + email delivery (SendGrid).
  • Optional: dashboard showing deadlines, document status, next steps.

5. Target Launch States (Judicial Foreclosure)

Focus first on judicial foreclosure states — where the lender must file a lawsuit and the homeowner files a formal answer — because those have the most document-prep need.

Tier States Rationale
Tier 1 — launch FL, NY, NJ, IL, OH Highest foreclosure volume + judicial process = most paperwork
Tier 2 — expand PA, SC, IN, CT, LA, MA Strong judicial foreclosure states, moderate volume
Tier 3 — later Non-judicial states (CA, TX, GA, etc.) Less court paperwork, more lender-communication focus
Why judicial states first: In judicial foreclosure, the homeowner must file a formal answer with the court within 20–30 days or risk default judgment. There is a clear, urgent document need — an "answer" to a "complaint" — that maps directly onto the eviction-defense architecture we've already built (answer + defenses + motions + fee waivers).

7. Compliance Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

Critical — learn from foreclosure rescue scams: The foreclosure space has a history of bad actors promising to "save homes" and taking money without delivering. Regulators and state AGs are aggressive. This product must be unambiguously self-help.
  • No lender contact: The platform never calls, emails, or negotiates with any lender, servicer, or their counsel.
  • No court filings: The platform never files documents with any court. The customer downloads and files pro se.
  • No legal advice: The platform presents options in plain English; the homeowner selects their own defenses and strategy.
  • No guarantees: The platform does not promise any outcome — no "we'll save your home," no "stop foreclosure."
  • Clear disclaimers: Every page: "Self-help document preparation. Not a law firm. Not legal advice. Not a foreclosure rescue service."
  • Transparent pricing: Flat fee disclosed before payment. No percentage of equity, no "rescue fee," no monthly subscriptions that imply ongoing representation.
  • Attorney referral option: If the case is complex or the homeowner wants representation, the platform can refer to a licensed foreclosure defense attorney.
